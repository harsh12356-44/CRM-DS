1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This document serves as the authoritative blueprint for the creation of the public-facing commercial marketing website for HRM Pilot. The primary objective of this website is to market and sell the HRM Pilot workforce management platform to external enterprise organizations, multi-branch corporations, and scaling agencies.

Strategic Objective: Deliver an interactive, animated, high-converting digital presence that positions HRM Pilot as an ultra-modern, zero-disruption workforce operating system with transparent, modular pricing.

4. Detailed Module Specifications for Website Messaging

A. Core HRM Base Platform (Baseline Subscription)

  • Multi-Company Tenant Workspaces: Allows holding companies and conglomerates to operate multiple distinct sister brands/subsidiaries within a single deployment with zero data leakage. Super Admins switch workspaces in 1 click.
  • Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict 4-tier compartmentalization across Super Admin, Company Admin, Department Manager, and Employee.
  • Branch-Specific Shift Schedules: Eliminates shift headaches by assigning customized daily targets per branch (e.g. Main HQ 8h shift with 1 Sunday off vs Marketing 9h shift with Saturday & Sunday off).
  • Automated Leave Quotas: Configurable Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), and Privilege Leave (PL) quotas with multi-tier manager approval workflows.
  • Company Notices & Policy Repo: Centralized distribution of operational announcements and employee handbooks.

B. Paid Add-On 1: Autonomous Time Tracker & Biometrics

  • Live Shift Timer: Precision desktop and web clock-in timer running real-time seconds counter with instant multi-device synchronization.
  • Smart Break Engine: Explicit lunch and tea break modes that automatically pause the net working minutes counter to guarantee accurate target compliance.
  • Hardware Biometric Integration: Sub-second API/socket sync with office fingerprint and facial recognition scanners (eSSL, ZKTeco, Matrix, Hikvision).
  • Overtime & Idle Surveillance: Flags shift discrepancies and computes billable overtime hours past the daily shift target.
  • Audit-Proof Punch Logs: Every punch is cryptographically stamped with employee ID, device ID, IP address, and exact timestamp.

C. Paid Add-On 2: Automated Payroll & Salary Processing

  • Dynamic CTC Structures: Configurable earnings (Basic, HRA, Special Allowance) and statutory deductions (Provident Fund, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS).
  • Attendance-Linked Loss-of-Pay (LOP): Directly ingests attendance logs from the Time Tracker to calculate unpaid absences and half-day penalties with zero human calculation error.
  • 1-Click Payslip Generation: Automatically produces password-protected PDF payslips and distributes them to staff employee portals.
  • Bank Batch Dispatch: Generates ready-to-upload NEFT/RTGS CSV and Excel transfer files compatible with major enterprise banks (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Chase).

6. Technical & Frontend Design Specifications

  • Color Palette & Design Tokens:
    • Base Background: Obsidian Dark (#06090f, #0c121e)
    • Card Surface: Glassmorphic Frosted (rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.65) with backdrop-filter: blur(14px))
    • Primary Accent: Royal Indigo (#6366f1) with glowing box-shadow (rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4))
    • Secondary Accent: Neon Cyan (#06b6d4 / #38bdf8) and Emerald (#10b981)
    • Subtle Borders: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)
  • Typography Hierarchy:
    • Display / Headings: Outfit (weights: 700, 800)
    • Body / Interface: Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter (weights: 400, 500, 600)
    • Numbers & Digital Timers: Outfit with letter-spacing: 0.05em and neon text-shadow
  • Interactive JavaScript Logic Requirements:
    • updateLiveClocks(): Updates all timers every 1,000ms with system zero-padded hours, minutes, and seconds.
    • switchSimTab(tabId): Toggles active CSS class between simulator panels with smooth opacity transitions.
    • updateCalculator(): Reads headcount and hours sliders; computes savings using: Savings = (Hours * 0.82 * $35) + (Headcount * $12).
    • updateCustomStack(): Ingests base platform rate + checked add-on rates (Time Tracker $1.50, Payroll $2.00) adjusted for monthly vs annual billing.
    • handleDemoSubmit(e): Intercepts form submit, reads email, hides inputs, and displays confirmed VIP booking state.
  • Reference Codebase Assets:
    • Root Standalone Preview: hrm-pilot-preview.html
    • Next.js Web Asset: public/hrm-pilot-preview.html
    • Preview Route: src/app/preview/page.tsx (redirects to preview)
