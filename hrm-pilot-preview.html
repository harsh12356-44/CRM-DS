Answers to Frequent Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about implementing HRM Pilot for your company.

How does the Multi-Company Tenant architecture work?
HRM Pilot provides zero-disruption multi-tenancy. A holding company or parent organization can create separate company profiles (e.g. Digital Suncity, Sister Agencies, Subsidiary LLPs). Each space has completely isolated employee directories, shift rules, and manager assignments, while the Super Admin can switch workspaces in 1 click without logging out.
Can we configure different working hours for different branches?
Yes! That is one of HRM Pilot's core strengths. For example, your Main HQ can be set to an 8-hour shift target (420m work + 60m break) with 1 weekly off (Sunday), while your SEO or Marketing branch can be set to 9 hours (480m work + 60m break) with 2 weekly offs (Saturday & Sunday).
Can HRM Pilot integrate with our existing biometric fingerprint/face scanners?
Absolutely. HRM Pilot supports direct API/socket synchronization with popular biometric manufacturers including eSSL, ZKTeco, Matrix, and Hikvision. As soon as an employee punches their finger or face at the turnstile, the punch registers on the HRM Pilot cloud database within milliseconds.
How fast can our company migrate our existing employee database?
Our zero-disruption CSV importer allows onboarding hundreds of employees in less than 30 minutes. You can import existing employee IDs, branch allocations, leave allowances, and past attendance histories without losing a single record.
Is our company workforce data secure and isolated?
Yes. All communications are protected with TLS 1.3 and military-grade AES-256 database encryption at rest. We enforce strict role-based access control (RBAC), automatic session revocation, and immutable audit logs.
